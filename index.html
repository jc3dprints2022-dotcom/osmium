Hardware · Front

Full-size controls.

TMR thumbsticks that don't drift, Hall-effect analog triggers, two back buttons, a gyro and dual haptic motors. The sticks sit in recessed wells so they don't catch in a pocket.

Hardware · Top

Heat goes up and out.

Air leaves through the top edge, away from your hands. Bumpers and triggers stay inside the profile. A 10 Gbps USB-C port sits up top for accessories.

Hardware · Back

One driver opens it.

Six T5 screws hold the bead-blasted aluminium back. Battery, SSD, fan and stick modules are all replaceable. The intake has a removable dust filter.

Hardware · Bottom

Ports where docks expect them.

USB4 at 40 Gbps on the bottom edge handles charging, DisplayPort 2.1 and docks. There is a real 3.5 mm headphone jack and stereo speakers either side.
